What we do

From one limb to a whole lot.

Every service below comes straight from Boozer's own list. Not sure what your tree needs? Call and talk it through with Cain.

01

Tree removal

Big pines, oaks, dead and leaning trees, taken down safely, close to houses, fences and pools.

02

Tree trimming

Heavy limbs over the house or the pool cut back with care, and trees shaped so they stay where they belong.

03

Limbing

Limbs taken off the trunk to clear a roofline, raise a canopy, or get a tree ready to come down.

04

Top working

Working a tree from the top down, piece by piece, when there's no room to drop it in one go.

05

Complex rigging

Ropes and rigging to lower heavy wood slowly over roofs, fences and gardens instead of letting it fall.

06

Stump grinding

Stumps ground down so the lawn can grow back where the tree used to be.

07

Lot clearing

Overgrown lots cleared out for a build, a fence line, a new yard, or a fresh start.

08

Demo

Demolition work, too. Tell Cain what needs to come down and he'll walk you through it.
