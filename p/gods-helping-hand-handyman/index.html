Rot & water damage

What’s behind the trim matters most.

Soft fascia, a spongy spot in the floor, a step that gives a little. Water damage rarely stays small. We open it up, replace the rotted wood, and close it back up so it’s solid again.

  1. Open it upFind where the water got in and how far the rot goes.
  2. Replace the rotted woodSoft, rotted boards and framing come out; sound lumber goes in.
  3. Close it back upFascia, soffit, siding or floor finished so it looks like it belongs.
