Paver restoration

Clean. Sand. Seal.

Faded, gritty pavers don't need replacing. They need three steps, done in the right order, by people who have specialized in paver sealing for 10 years.

Clean.

A surface cleaner and pressure wash strip off the dirt and grime that turn pavers dull and gray.

Sand.

Fresh sand goes back into the joints so every paver sits snug again.

Seal.

A sealer coat locks the sand in place and brings the color back to the surface.
