Late fall & winter

Leaves are down. Now look up.

With the leaves gone you can see every branch: the dead limb over the roof, the split trunk, the tree that's been leaning a little more each year. It's a good time to have someone take a look before winter weather hits it.

  • Dead or hanging limbs over the house, garage or driveway
  • Trees leaning toward the house or the fence
  • Old stumps you want gone before spring
