Ice-dam season

When the thaw finds its way inside.

Snow on a Pittsburgh roof melts, refreezes at the edge, and backs up. When those ice dams let go, the water can end up in your ceilings and walls. That’s where SelRahc comes in.

    Prep before the first cut

    Drywall is dusty work, so the room gets protected and prepped before the job starts.

  3. III

    Finish and paint

    Patched, finished and painted, so the wall reads clean again.
