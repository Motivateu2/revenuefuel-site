Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

The short version: we collect what you send us so we can answer you, build what you asked for, or get you processing bids. We don't sell your information. Ask us anytime and we'll delete it.

Who we are

Revenue Fuel LLC ("Revenue Fuel," "we," "us") helps local businesses with websites, marketing and card-processing options. This policy covers revenuefuel.ai, our forms (including forms on Facebook and Instagram), and messages you send us.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Business details: your business name and type, your website or social media links, and anything you tell us about your goals or timeline.
  • Files and photos you send us so we can build a website preview.
  • Processing figures you choose to share, such as monthly card sales and fees.
  • Basic site data: like most websites, our host may record standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) when you visit.
  • Visit counts: we use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service that sets no cookies, to count page visits and which buttons get tapped. It does not identify you, and nothing you type into a form on our pages is recorded.

How we use it

  • To reply to you by text, phone or email about the request you made.
  • To build and send the website preview or proposal you asked for.
  • To get you processing bids, if you ask for them.
  • To follow up about that request. You can tell us to stop at any time.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only:

  • with service providers that help us run the business (for example email, website hosting and payment processing), and only for that purpose;
  • with a card processor, and only if you ask us to get you a bid or you choose to switch;
  • when the law requires it.

Facebook and Instagram lead forms

If you fill out one of our forms on Facebook or Instagram, Meta passes us the answers you submit. Meta's own use of your data is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while we're working with you, and for as long as we need it to answer your request or meet legal and accounting requirements. Processing statements you send are deleted within 30 days unless you become a client.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email hello@revenuefuel.ai and we'll take care of it.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
